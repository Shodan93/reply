Bordered content block, the base unit for service tiles, job cards, and article previews.

```jsx
<Card eyebrow="Service" title="Cloud Computing" accent="var(--teal-500)">
  Migration, Betrieb und Skalierung...
</Card>
```

Variants: `bordered` (default, hairline border, flat), `elevated` (shadow, no border, for overlays/featured), `inverse` (ink-filled, for dark section cards). `accent` adds a 4px top rule in one mosaic color.
